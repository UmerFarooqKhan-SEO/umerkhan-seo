SEO Case Study · Education & Training

Inspire London College

SEO strategy focused on Technical SEO, keyword research, on-page optimization, content strategy, internal linking, schema and organic search visibility.

Project Overview

Inspire London College is a UK-based education and training website offering professional qualifications and online courses.

The SEO work focused on improving organic visibility, strengthening technical SEO, targeting relevant search queries, optimizing course and category pages and developing a stronger internal website structure.

SEO Objectives

Organic Visibility

Improve visibility across relevant education, qualification and training searches.

Keyword Rankings

Identify and target relevant search queries with realistic ranking opportunities.

Technical SEO

Identify technical issues affecting crawling, indexing and website performance.

Content Growth

Build content around relevant topics and search intent.

Technical SEO

Technical SEO analysis was used to identify opportunities affecting crawling, indexing, performance and overall website health.

  • Technical SEO audits
  • Crawl analysis
  • Indexation analysis
  • XML sitemap review
  • Robots.txt review
  • Canonical URL analysis
  • Broken link analysis
  • Redirect analysis
  • Duplicate content checks
  • Core Web Vitals analysis

Keyword Research

Keyword research focused on understanding how potential learners search for qualifications, courses and professional training opportunities.

Course Keywords

Keywords related to specific courses and training programs.

Qualification Keywords

Search terms related to diplomas, qualifications and professional certifications.

Informational Keywords

Questions and informational searches related to health, social care and professional training.

Commercial Keywords

Search queries showing potential interest in enrolling in relevant courses.

On-Page SEO

Course and category pages were optimized to improve relevance, search intent alignment and user experience.

  • Title tag optimization
  • Meta description optimization
  • Heading structure
  • Keyword optimization
  • Content improvements
  • Image optimization
  • URL optimization
  • Internal linking
  • Search intent alignment
  • Structured data optimization

Content Strategy

Content opportunities were identified around relevant education, qualification and health and social care topics.

The content strategy focused on search intent, long-tail opportunities, topical relevance and creating useful supporting content that could strengthen important course and category pages.

Internal Linking & Site Structure

Internal links were used to connect relevant courses, categories and supporting content.

Course Pages

Connected related qualification and course pages.

Supporting Content

Linked informational content to relevant commercial pages.

Topic Clusters

Strengthened relationships between related topics.

SEO Tools

Google Search Console

Search performance, indexing and query analysis.

Semrush

Keyword research, competitor analysis and ranking analysis.

Screaming Frog

Technical crawling and website auditing.

PageSpeed Insights

Performance and Core Web Vitals analysis.

Key Learnings

  • Technical SEO and content strategy need to work together.
  • Search intent is important when targeting course-related keywords.
  • Internal linking can strengthen topical relationships across a website.
  • Regular technical audits help identify SEO opportunities.
  • SEO performance should be measured using multiple data sources.
