SEO Case Study · Education & Training

Inspire London College

SEO strategy focused on technical SEO, keyword research, on-page optimization, content strategy, internal linking and organic search growth for a UK education and training website.

Project Overview

Inspire London College is a UK-based education and training website offering professional qualifications and courses across areas including health and social care, business, management and other vocational subjects.

The SEO strategy focused on improving organic visibility, strengthening keyword rankings, increasing relevant search traffic and building stronger topical relevance across course and qualification pages.

SEO Objectives

Organic Growth

Increase relevant organic traffic from users searching for professional qualifications and courses.

Keyword Rankings

Improve rankings for commercially valuable course, qualification and education-related keywords.

Topical Authority

Strengthen topical relevance across courses, qualifications and supporting educational content.

Search Visibility

Increase impressions and visibility across Google search results.

Keyword Research & Strategy

Keyword research focused on identifying relevant education, qualification, diploma and course searches with strong informational and commercial intent.

Course Keywords

Targeted searches related to individual courses and training programmes.

Qualification Keywords

Focused on searches for UK qualifications, diplomas and certification programmes.

Long-Tail Keywords

Targeted specific searches with clear learner intent.

Commercial Keywords

Prioritised searches showing potential enrolment and course interest.

Technical SEO

Technical SEO work focused on improving crawlability, indexation, site structure and overall search engine accessibility.

  • Technical SEO audits
  • Google Search Console analysis
  • Crawl and indexation analysis
  • XML sitemap review
  • Robots.txt review
  • Canonical URL analysis
  • Broken link analysis
  • Redirect analysis
  • Page speed analysis
  • Core Web Vitals review
  • Structured data and schema implementation

On-Page SEO

Course and qualification pages were optimized to better align page content with search intent and target keywords.

  • SEO title optimization
  • Meta description optimization
  • Heading structure optimization
  • Keyword placement
  • Course page optimization
  • Qualification page optimization
  • Content structure improvements
  • Image optimization
  • Internal linking
  • Schema markup optimization

Content Strategy

Content planning was developed around learner search intent, qualification-related questions, course topics and supporting informational searches.

The strategy connected informational content with relevant course and qualification pages through contextual internal linking, helping build stronger topical relationships across the website.

Website Structure & Internal Linking

Internal linking was used to create stronger relationships between qualification categories, individual courses and supporting content.

Category → Course

Connected qualification and subject categories with relevant course pages.

Blog → Course

Used informational content to support important commercial course pages.

Course → Related Course

Strengthened navigation and topical relationships between related programmes.

Structured Data & Schema

Structured data opportunities were reviewed and implemented where appropriate to help search engines better understand course and qualification information.

  • Course-related structured data
  • Educational qualification markup opportunities
  • Breadcrumb structured data
  • Organization information
  • Website structured data

SEO Tools Used

Google Search Console

Search performance, indexing, queries and ranking analysis.

Semrush

Keyword research, competitor analysis and ranking tracking.

Screaming Frog

Technical crawling, site auditing and issue identification.

Google Analytics

Organic traffic and user behaviour analysis.

SEO Results & Performance

The SEO campaign generated substantial growth in organic search visibility, clicks and average search position.

+35.6%

Organic clicks increased from 26.4K to 35.8K.

+72.5%

Search impressions increased from 3.24M to 5.59M.

27.6 → 10.2

Average search position improved by 17.4 positions.

0.6% → 0.8%

CTR increased by 0.2 percentage points.

Key outcome:

Organic clicks increased by 35.6%, while search impressions increased by 72.5%.

Average search position improved from 27.6 to 10.2, representing a 17.4-position improvement and significantly stronger first-page visibility.

CTR also improved from 0.6% to 0.8%.

Key Learnings

  • Strong keyword targeting can significantly improve organic visibility for education websites.
  • Course and qualification pages benefit from clear search intent alignment.
  • Internal linking helps strengthen topical relationships across related courses and educational content.
  • Technical SEO provides an important foundation for crawling, indexing and organic growth.
  • Combining technical SEO, content and on-page optimization can produce measurable organic search improvements.
